What your team will learn

  1. Open assigned leads and check for an existing customer record.
  2. Verify carrier information with SAFER and FMCSA.
  3. Record the conversation and follow-up. Outbound calls require DNC clearance.
  4. Complete the customer application, choose services and save a draft.
  5. Submit the application and prepare an itemized quote for owner review.
  6. Request secure checkout after owner approval. Customers enter their own payment details.
  7. Understand how verified payment closes a sale and starts customer onboarding.
  8. Review activity and follow up without creating duplicate accounts.

A browser redirect alone is not payment confirmation. Representatives cannot mark a real sale paid.
